Frequently Asked

Everything you wanted to know.

How many capsules does one day use?
Each daily sheet contains 12 capsules — a six-step day routine and a six-step night routine: makeup remover oil, facial cleanser, toner, essence, eye cream, face cream, and primer. Each capsule is a single full-face dose, used once and discarded for maximum hygiene and freshness.
Do Lumière capsules meet TSA liquid limits?
Yes. Each capsule holds a fraction of a millilitre and is individually sealed, placing it far below the 3.4 oz / 100 ml carry-on liquid limit. Most travellers carry a full sheet without using their quart bag at all, though the officer on duty always has final discretion.
Is it safe for sensitive skin?
Lumière is formulated for all skin types, including sensitive. Each capsule is hermetically sealed to prevent oxidation and contamination, which is especially important for reactive complexions.
Who is Lumière formulated for?
Lumière is a unisex formula — designed for both men and women, around the hydration, brightening, anti-aging, and care priorities of a complete daily ritual. The capsules work across all skin types: sensitive, oily, dry, normal, and deeply pigmented.
Where is Lumière made?
Lumière is designed and formulated in Maryland, USA, and manufactured at a GMP-certified facility in China using premium, traceable ingredients. Each batch is independently tested for purity and potency.
Do I have to use every capsule on the sheet?
No. The sheet is designed as a complete ritual, but each capsule is also a standalone treatment. Use only what your skin asks for — the rest stay sealed and fresh.
What's the shelf life?
Sealed skincare capsules stay fresh for three years from the manufactured date. Because each dose is hermetically sealed and used once, there is no oxidation or contamination between uses — unlike a jar or pump bottle opened daily.
How much liquid can you bring on a plane?
You may bring liquids in containers of 3.4 ounces (100 ml) or less, and all containers must fit inside one quart-sized clear resealable bag — one bag per passenger. The printed container size is what counts, not how much product remains inside it. Full TSA guide →
Why does skin get dry on planes?
Cabin humidity drops to roughly 10–20 percent, drier than most deserts. That pulls moisture from skin faster than it can be replaced, which is why hydration and a barrier-sealing moisturizer matter more in flight than any other step.
