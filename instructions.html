How to Use

Your ritual, morning & night.

Each Lumière sheet contains your full day of skincare. Here's exactly which capsule to use, when, and in what order — morning and evening.

♂

A note for men: the Makeup Remover (night) and Primer (day) capsules are designed for makeup wearers and can be skipped entirely. Your routine simply starts at Cleanser — everything else works exactly the same.

☀ Day Box
☀ Morning · 6 Steps

Daytime Use

Wake up your skin in six light steps — fresh, hydrated, and ready for makeup or SPF.

1
Cleanser

Massage the gentle foam over damp skin to lift overnight oil buildup. Rinse with lukewarm water.

2
Toner

Pat onto clean skin with fingertips — resets your pH and preps the canvas for everything that follows.

3
Essence

Press the concentrated treatment into cheeks, forehead, and chin. Targets dullness and uneven tone.

4
Eye Cream

Dab gently around the orbital bone with your ring finger — never rub. De-puffs and brightens.

5
Face Cream

Smooth upward and outward to lock in every layer beneath. Your daytime moisture shield.

6
Primer Men can skip

Apply last to blur pores and smooth the surface — the perfect base for makeup or SPF.

☾ Evening · 6 Steps

Nighttime Use

Repair while you sleep — the full ritual, working overnight.

1
Makeup Remover Men can skip

The tear-free oil dissolves makeup, SPF, and the day. Massage over dry skin, then rinse.

2
Cleanser

A second cleanse for a perfectly clear canvas — clears any remaining residue from pores.

3
Toner

Rebalance and re-flood skin with hydration after cleansing.

4
Essence

Repairing antioxidants get to work overnight, when skin regenerates fastest.

5
Eye Cream

Targets dark circles and dry fine lines while you sleep.

6
Face Cream

A nourishing seal that locks in the entire routine for deep overnight repair.

☾ Night Box
Opening a Capsule

Four seconds, no scissors.

Each capsule is designed for effortless one-handed opening — even in a hotel bathroom at 6am.

01

Tear the Header

Pull off the strip at the top of the sheet to release the seal. Then divide the Day and Night Sheet into two halves.

02

Tear One Capsule

Detach today's capsule along the perforation — the rest stay sealed and fresh.

03

Peel the Corner

Each capsule has an easy-tear corner. Peel and it opens cleanly — no mess.

04

Apply & Discard

Use your finger or the top strip to scoop the perfect single dose into your palm, apply, and toss the empty shell.

Pro Tips

Get the most from every capsule.

Warm it up

Rub serums and creams between your fingertips for 2 seconds before applying — they absorb faster on warm skin.

Wait 30 seconds between steps

Give each layer a moment to absorb before the next. Thinnest to thickest is the golden rule.

Don't forget your neck

Bring toner, essence, and face cream down to your neck and décolletage — they age too.

One capsule = one full face

Each capsule is precisely dosed. If you have product left over, use it on the backs of your hands.

Store cool & dry

Keep unused sheets away from direct sunlight and heat. No refrigeration needed — capsules stay fresh for 3 years from the manufactured date.

SPF is your final morning step

After primer, always finish your daytime routine with sunscreen — your skincare's best friend.
