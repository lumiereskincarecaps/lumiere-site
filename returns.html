Eligibility

What can & can't be returned.

Clear, fair, and exactly what you'd want a luxury brand to do.

Eligible for Return

  • Unopened capsule sheets within 30 days of delivery
  • Opened sheets within 30 days under the Skin-Try Guarantee
  • Damaged or defective items at any time (we'll cover return shipping & replace immediately)
  • Items shipped in error
  • Gifts (refund issued as store credit to the gift recipient)

Not Eligible

  • Items returned more than 30 days after delivery
  • Heavily used sheets (more than 50% of capsules used) returned outside the Skin-Try window
  • Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout
  • Gift cards & digital products
  • Items damaged through misuse or storage outside recommended conditions

Returns FAQ

Everything else you might be wondering.

How do I start a return?
Log into your Lumière account or visit our self-service returns portal at your account portal. Enter your order number and email — you'll get a prepaid return label by email within 24 hrs.
Do I have to pay for return shipping?
Not for U.S. orders — return shipping is on us, always. International returns are paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged or was sent in error.
Can I return an opened sheet?
Yes — under our 30-day Skin-Try Guarantee, you can return an opened sheet within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Even if you've used a few capsules.
How long does a refund take?
Refunds are processed within 3 business days of your return arriving at our studio. From there, your bank or card issuer typically takes another 5–10 business days to post the funds.
What if my product arrived damaged?
Email care@lumiereskincarecaps.com within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the damage. We'll send a replacement immediately at no cost — no need to return the damaged item.
Can I exchange instead of refunding?
Yes — and exchanges are even easier. We waive shipping in both directions and dispatch your new item within 24 hours of approval. Just choose "Exchange" instead of "Refund" in the returns portal.
I received the wrong item — what do I do?
We're sorry for the mixup. Email us with your order number and a photo of what you received. We'll send the correct item by overnight shipping at no cost, plus a prepaid label to return the wrong one.
Can I return a gift?
Yes. The original purchaser won't be notified — refunds for gifts are issued as store credit to the gift recipient. Just enter the order number and the recipient's email in the returns portal.
What about international returns?
International orders can be returned within 30 days, but return shipping is paid by the customer (unless the item arrived damaged or was sent in error). Customs duties paid at checkout are non-refundable.
How does the Skin-Try Guarantee work, exactly?
Open the sheet, try the ritual for up to 30 days, and if it isn't right for you — for any reason — send what's left back for a full refund. We mean it: no restocking fees, no usage limits, no awkward forms.
