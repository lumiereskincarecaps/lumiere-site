Shop FAQ

Quick answers before you click "Add to Bag."

Which pack should I start with?
If you're new to Lumière, the 3-Day Trial is the lowest-commitment way to try the full ritual. The 7-Day Pack is our best-seller and delivers the lowest per-day price. Want the sponges, too? The Deluxe Set is your move.
What's actually in the Deluxe Set?
A full 7-Day Capsule Pack (49 capsules) and our Exfoliating & Facial Sponges (set of 2) — for $10 less than buying both separately.
Can I subscribe and save on every pack?
Subscribe & Save is available on the 7-Day Pack. You'll save 10% on every recurring order and can pause, skip, or cancel anytime from your account.
What's in a daily sheet?
Each daily sheet contains 12 capsules — a six-step day routine and a six-step night routine: makeup remover oil, facial cleanser, toner, essence, eye cream, face cream, and primer. The full skincare experience in a single, sealed sheet.
Are the Sponges sold separately?
Yes — the Exfoliating & Facial Sponges (set of 2) are available as a standalone for $30. But they're a better deal inside the Deluxe Set, where you save $10 vs. buying both separately.
What if it doesn't work for my skin?
Every Lumière purchase is backed by our 30-Day Skin-Try Guarantee — if it isn't right for you, send it back (even if you've opened it) for a full refund. See our returns page for full details.
