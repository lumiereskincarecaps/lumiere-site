Our Story

Born in a hotel bathroom in 2024.

The idea for Lumière came on a delayed flight to Lisbon. After a 14-hour transit, three transfers, and one half-leaked toner bottle, our founder Claire looked at her destroyed toiletry bag and asked the obvious question: why is no one doing skincare in single doses?

The pharmaceutical industry has done blister-pack precision dosing for decades. The supplement world has it. The skincare aisle was still pumping serums out of hand-pump bottles that oxidize the moment air hits them.

So we built it. A complete day-and-night skincare ritual — six steps in the morning, six at night — sealed into 12 individual capsules in a single sheet. No oxidation. No spillage. No half-empty bottles taking up real estate on a shelf you'll only see twice a week.

From a tiny formulation studio in Gaithersburg, Maryland, Lumière now ships to over 60 countries. The team is still small. The mission has not changed.

The Journey

From hotel bathroom to 60 countries.

2024 · January
The first prototype

Claire builds the first capsule prototype in her kitchen using off-the-shelf gel caps and a pipette. The concept holds.

2024 · June
Maryland studio opens

Lumière secures its first formulation studio in Gaithersburg, MD. First production run: 200 sheets.

2025 · March
Public launch

The 3-Day Trial and 7-Day packs ship to early customers. First wholesale partnership with a boutique hotel in Brooklyn.

2025 · September
International expansion

Lumière begins shipping to Europe, the UK, Canada, and Japan. Subscribe & Save launches.

2026 · April
The Deluxe Set

The Exfoliating & Facial Sponges join the line. The Deluxe Set debuts as our first multi-product offering.
