1. Who we are

Lumière Skincare Capsules ("Lumière," "we," "us") operates lumiereskincarecaps.com and sells single-use skincare capsules directly to customers. We are based in Gaithersburg, Maryland, USA. This policy explains what we do with personal information collected through our website, our customer portal, and our customer service channels.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Order details: name, email address, shipping address, billing address, phone number, and what you bought.
  • Account details: if you create a portal account — your name, email, password (stored in encrypted form), and any address or phone number you save.
  • Messages: anything you send through our contact form, return requests, or emails to our care team.
  • Marketing preferences: your email address if you subscribe to The Letter, and whether you've opted in or out.
  • Ambassador and affiliate applications: your name, contact details, social handles, and payment details (PayPal, Zelle, or bank information) if you join our program.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages viewed, links clicked, time on site, referring website.
  • Device data: browser type, operating system, screen size, approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see section 6.

Payment information

We never see or store your full card number. Payments are processed by Stripe, which handles card data directly under PCI-DSS standards. We receive only a confirmation, the last four digits, and the card brand.

3. How we use your information

PurposeWhat this means
Fulfilling ordersTaking payment, shipping your products, sending tracking and receipts
Customer supportAnswering questions, handling returns and refunds

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Legal bases (UK/EU visitors)

Where GDPR applies, we process personal data on these bases: contract (to fulfil your order), consent (marketing emails and non-essential cookies), legitimate interests (site improvement, fraud prevention, security), and legal obligation (tax and accounting records).

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who need it to do their job for us, under contracts that limit how they may use it:

  • Payment processing — Stripe
  • Shipping and fulfilment — carriers such as USPS, UPS and DHL, and our shipping platforms
  • Email — our transactional and marketing email providers
  • Hosting and infrastructure — our website host and database provider
  • Analytics — Google Analytics, where enabled

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or as part of a business sale or merger — in which case we'll tell you before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

6. Cookies and tracking

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. We use:

  • Essential cookies — keep items in your cart and keep you signed in. The site can't work without these.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how the site is used, in aggregate.
  • Marketing cookies — used only if advertising is active, to measure ad performance.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will break checkout and sign-in. We honour Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals where required by law.

Our site also uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart and preferences. That data stays on your device.

7. How long we keep information

  • Order and tax records: seven years, as required for accounting.
  • Account information: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Marketing lists: until you unsubscribe.
  • Support messages: up to three years.
  • Analytics data: up to 26 months.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy
  • Correct information that's wrong or incomplete
  • Delete your information (subject to records we must legally keep)
  • Opt out of marketing at any time
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — note we don't do either
  • Ask us to restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To exercise any of these, email care@lumiereskincarecaps.com. We'll respond within 30 days (45 days for California requests, where an extension may apply). We may need to verify your identity first. Portal account holders can also download their own data directly from the Account tab.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): the categories above describe the personal information we collect, why, and who we share it with. We have not sold or shared personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising in the past twelve months.

UK/EU residents: you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

9. How we protect your information

We use HTTPS encryption across the site, restrict who on our team can access customer data, and rely on established providers (like Stripe) for payment handling. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

10. Children's privacy

Our products and site are intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

11. International transfers

We're based in the United States, and information you give us is processed there. If you're in the UK, EU, or elsewhere, your information will be transferred to the US, protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where the change materially affects you, notify you by email or a notice on the site.
